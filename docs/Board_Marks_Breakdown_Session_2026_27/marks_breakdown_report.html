CRITICAL CURRICULUM RULE — L5 (EVALUATE) & L6 (CREATE) EXCEPTION POLICY:
In all secondary and higher-secondary board frameworks, individual chapters have defined marks ranges [0 to Max] across Bloom's levels. Foundational and recall-heavy chapters (e.g., Our Environment, Acids & Bases, Human Eye) maintain a strict weightage of 0 marks for L5 and L6. Higher-order skills (L5 & L6) are strictly restricted to designated Competency-Based / Case-Study / Multi-concept Problem units (e.g., Life Processes, Electricity, Optics, Java OOP Synthesis).

1. Executive Summary & Cognitive Domain Framework

School education boards in India have transitioned from rote evaluation to Competency-Based Assessment (CBE) aligned with Bloom's Revised Taxonomy (Anderson & Krathwohl). Each session releases updated Curriculum Documents and Sample Question Papers (SQP).
