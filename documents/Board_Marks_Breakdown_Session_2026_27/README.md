## Summary of Subject Marks Breakdowns

### 1. CBSE Class 10 Subjects
- **Science (Code 086)**: 80 Marks Theory (5 Units, 13 Chapters) + 20 Marks Internal Assessment
  - Unit 1: Chemical Substances (25M)
  - Unit 2: World of Living (25M)
  - Unit 3: Natural Phenomena (12M)
  - Unit 4: Effects of Current (13M)
  - Unit 5: Natural Resources (5M)
  - *L5/L6 Exception:* Strict restriction to Ch 5 (Life Processes), Ch 9 (Light), and Ch 11 (Electricity). 0 marks in all other 10 chapters.
- **Mathematics Standard (Code 041)**: 80 Marks Theory + 20 Marks Internal Assessment
  - Number Systems (6M), Algebra (20M), Coordinate Geometry (6M), Geometry (15M), Trigonometry (12M), Mensuration (10M), Statistics & Probability (11M).
- **Social Science (Code 087)**: 80 Marks Theory + 20 Marks Internal Assessment
  - History (20M: 18 theory + 2 map), Geography (20M: 17 theory + 3 map), Civics (20M), Economics (20M).
- **English Language & Literature (Code 184)**: 80 Marks Theory + 20 Marks ALS
  - Section A Reading Skills (20M), Section B Writing & Grammar (20M), Section C Literature (40M).
- **Information Technology (Code 402)**: 50 Marks Theory + 50 Marks Practical
  - Part A Employability Skills (10M), Part B Subject Specific Skills (40M).

### 2. CBSE Class 12 Core Subjects
- **Physics (Code 042)**: 70 Marks Theory + 30 Marks Practical
  - Electrostatics & Current (16M), Magnetism & EMI/AC (17M), EM Waves & Optics (18M), Dual Nature & Atoms/Nuclei (12M), Electronic Devices (7M).
- **Chemistry (Code 043)**: 70 Marks Theory + 30 Marks Practical
  - 10 Units: Solutions (7M), Electrochemistry (9M), Kinetics (7M), d- & f-Block (7M), Coordination (7M), Haloalkanes (6M), Alcohols/Phenols (6M), Aldehydes/Ketones (8M), Amines (6M), Biomolecules (7M).
- **Computer Science (Code 083)**: 70 Marks Theory + 30 Marks Practical
  - Computational Thinking & Python (40M), Computer Networks (10M), Database Management SQL (20M).
- **Mathematics (Code 041)**: 80 Marks Theory + 20 Marks Internal
  - Calculus (35M), Vectors & 3D (14M), Algebra (10M), Relations & Functions (8M), Probability (8M), Linear Programming (5M).

### 3. ICSE Class 10 Subjects
- **Science Papers (Physics, Chemistry, Biology)**: 80 Marks Theory (Sec A: 40M Compulsory, Sec B: 40M Choice) + 20 Marks Internal each.
- **Computer Applications (Group III)**: 100 Marks Theory (Sec A: 40M Compulsory, Sec B: 60M Programming) + 100 Marks Internal/Practical.

### 4. PSEB Class 10 (ਪੰਜਾਬ ਸਕੂਲ ਸਿੱਖਿਆ ਬੋਰਡ) Subjects
